2. CANONICAL ORIGIN LABELS
========================================================================================================================

PURPOSE:
- Define the exact source labels for all content entering or generated inside the system.
- Prevent ambiguous source authority.

CANONICAL ORIGIN LABELS:

1. system_policy
- Platform/system/security content.
- Highest authority.
- Cannot be overridden by anything lower.
- Must be versioned and hash-bound.

2. governance_policy
- Active L5 policy bundle, standards overlays, hard constraints, risk-tier rules, and safety requirements.
- Binding authority.
- Must be policy_hash-bound.

3. registry_config
- Approved agent/tool/prompt/connector/model definitions.
- Binding only for its declared scope.
- Must be registry_digest-bound.

4. developer_admin
- Governed implementation instructions and repo-local constraints.
- Binding only where compatible with system, governance policy, and registry.
- Must not override higher authority.

5. route_contract
- L0 RouteContract fields.
- Binding route metadata.
- Cannot be overwritten by user/retrieved/tool/human/model content.

6. capability_sandbox
- Capability token and sandbox envelope fields.
- Binding runtime authority scope.
- Cannot be widened by lower-authority content.

7. user_turn
- End-user task intent.
- Intent only.
- No policy authority.
- No registry authority.
- No tool/model/connector authority.
- No write authority.

8. retrieved
- C0 evidence or external source content.
- Data only.
- Never instruction.
- Must preserve source lineage and citation refs.

9. tool_output
- Browser/MCP/tool return.
- Data only until classified.
- Must preserve invocation refs.
- Must be scanned before entering context or arguments.

10. model_output
- Generated model text or tool-call proposal.
- Data/proposal only.
- Cannot widen authority.
- Cannot become policy.
- Cannot create new tool/model/connector authority.

11. human_review
- Human-entered approval, correction, modification, or SME/operator comment.
- Data only until re-cleared.
- Cannot directly mutate durable state.
- Cannot bypass L5, Exit, or UWG.

12. prior_artifact
- Cached answer, prior trace, old output, historical document, previous packet, old policy text, or old eval artifact.
- Data only until freshness and policy cleared.
- Cannot supply live authority without current binding.

13. external_untrusted
- Webpage, email, Slack, PDF, ticket, customer text, uploaded document, vendor docs, or arbitrary external content.
- Data only.
- Higher scrutiny for prompt-like content.

14. unknown
- Missing, malformed, ambiguous, or unsupported source.
- Untrusted by default.
- Must not enter authority-bearing slots.
