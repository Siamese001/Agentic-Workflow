 ── WHERE "DETERMINISTIC REPLAY" SITS ─────────────────────────────────────

   It is layer 3 — the MIDDLE of the tower. The two classic confusions:

   ✗ "replay is the foundation"  — no: it CANNOT EXIST until fixtures
                                    are pinned (1) and state is controlled (2)
   ✗ "replay is the goal"        — no: it's scaffolding FOR the suite (4)
                                    and change-control (5) above it

 ── FAILURE PROPAGATION (why order matters) ───────────────────────────────

   hole in 1 (drifting fixture)    → every layer above compares apples
                                     to oranges
   hole in 2 (one stray sticky     → "same input, different result" with
    note)                            no way to tell WHY → replay verdicts
                                     meaningless
   hole in 3 (no replay)           → suite must use live one-offs:
                                     slow, costly, noisy
   hole in 4 (no baseline)         → slow rot ships invisibly
   hole in side towers             → green results you cannot trust
