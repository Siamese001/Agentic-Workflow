C0.5 FINAL EVIDENCE CONTRACT, ZERO-LOSS OVERWRITE
Example ask:
"Find the current pet policy in Amit's lease and cite the exact clause."

CORE DISTINCTION
================

C0.1 plans the search.
C0.2 pulls candidate chunks and hydrates metadata.
C0.3 expands bounded graph relationships.
C0.4 shapes, reranks, trims, and stratifies evidence.
C0.5 verifies whether the shaped evidence is strong enough for Prompt Assembly.

C0.4 = ShapedEvidenceSet.
C0.5 = FinalEvidenceContract.
C0.5 asks: "Is this evidence strong enough, and what may Prompt Assembly pack?"

C0.5 does not fetch new chunks.
C0.5 does not broaden source scope.
C0.5 does not rerank again except for verification flags.
C0.5 does not answer.
C0.5 does not assemble prompts.
C0.5 does not execute.
C0.5 does not write L4.
C0.5 emits exactly one FinalEvidenceContract.
