# Rationale Quality Judge — Holdout Dataset

## Purpose of DS-R1

`rationale_judge_holdout.yaml` is the **ground-truth dataset** for calibrating and
validating `RationaleQualityJudge` in `apps_underwriting_ai/engines/judges/rationale_quality_judge.py`.

It exists to answer the question:
> "Does the judge assign scores that agree with qualified human underwriting analysts?"

Spearman rank correlation (judge scores vs. human scores) must meet:
- Global: ρ ≥ 0.80
- Per dimension: ρ ≥ 0.70

DS-R1 unblocks: DS-R2 (LLM judge), DS-R5 (calibration gate), DS-R6 (weekly report), DS-R8 (fail-closed default).

## Why This Must Be Human-Labeled

Synthetic scores (generated by Cascade or any model) are calibrated against the
deterministic scoring model — meaning they measure how well the judge agrees with
*itself*, not with human judgment. That is circular.

DS-R1 is complete **only** when:
1. `rationale_judge_holdout.yaml` contains ≥ 100 records, ≥ 20 per dimension.
2. Every record has a `labeler_id` identifying a real analyst or controlled alias.
3. Labels have been reviewed by at least one qualified underwriting analyst.
4. `tests/apps_underwriting_ai/test_ds_r1_holdout_dataset.py` passes.

Records with `labeler_id: synthetic_cascade_v1` (or any `synthetic_*` prefix) are
**excluded** from the DS-R1 acceptance count by the validator.

## No PII Rule

- `decision_id` must be an opaque identifier (e.g. `uw-holdout-evidence_sufficiency-001`).
- No applicant names, SSNs, phone numbers, email addresses, or geographic identifiers.
- `rationale_text` must be **anonymized or synthetic**. Real rationale text may be used
  only after stripping all PII per the compliance team's redaction checklist.
- The `prod_log_miner.py` tool (`tools/underwriting/prod_log_miner.py`) automates
  PII redaction for candidate examples mined from production logs.

## Labeling Rubric

### Dimensions

Each example targets exactly one of these five dimensions (`dim_id`):

| dim_id | What is being graded |
|---|---|
| `evidence_sufficiency` | Was enough evidence collected to support the decision? |
| `feature_derivation_correctness` | Were the risk features computed correctly from source data? |
| `policy_compliance` | Does the decision comply with all applicable policy thresholds? |
| `explainability` | Is the rationale text clear, specific, and sufficient for audit? |
| `fairness` | Was the decision free of protected-attribute influence; are adverse action codes correct? |

### Scoring Scale

Score a single dimension per record. Use the anchors below; intermediate values are allowed.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **1.0** | Excellent — fully correct, fully supported, no gaps |
| **0.75** | Mostly correct with minor gaps or minor imprecision |
| **0.5** | Mixed — partially supported or partially correct |
| **0.25** | Weak — materially incomplete, likely misleading, or partially incorrect |
| **0.0** | Wrong, unsupported, noncompliant, unfair, or unusable |

Fractional values (e.g. 0.38, 0.62) are valid. Use them when the rationale falls
between anchors.

### Dimension-Specific Guidance

#### evidence_sufficiency
- 1.0: All five evidence categories (financial, credit, collateral, relationship, policy) present and verified.
- 0.5: 2–3 categories present; remainder pending or absent.
- 0.0: Empty rationale or no evidence categories present.

#### feature_derivation_correctness
- 1.0: All `risk_*` features derived correctly from verified sources; no errors.
- 0.5: Partial feature set; some features missing or flagged with derivation uncertainty.
- 0.0: No features derived, or explicit incorrect derivation identified.

#### policy_compliance
- 1.0: Zero violations, all thresholds confirmed with margins; policy version cited.
- 0.5: Minor exception present but properly documented and approved.
- 0.0: Hard gate violated, or policy review not completed.

#### explainability
- 1.0: Long, specific rationale with quantified metrics, policy citations, and full evidence trail.
- 0.5: Adequate length but missing specific metrics or evidence references.
- 0.0: Empty, "N/A", single word, or completely non-specific ("Declined.").

#### fairness
- 1.0: Explicit confirmation no protected attributes used; adverse action codes cite only financial factors; ECOA satisfied.
- 0.5: Fairness screen ran but findings are ambiguous or a flag was raised.
- 0.0: Fairness review not completed, system error, or protected attributes referenced in rationale.

## Labeler Identity

- `labeler_id` must identify a real analyst or a **controlled alias** (e.g. `uw-analyst-A1`).
- The alias-to-person mapping is maintained offline by the compliance team.
- `labeler_id: synthetic_cascade_v1` is reserved for synthetic/dev records and
  is **blocked** by the DS-R1 validator.

## Analyst Labeling Workflow

1. Export candidate examples from `rationale_judge_holdout.synthetic_seed.yaml`
   or from `tools/underwriting/prod_log_miner.py --source <log_path>`.
2. Strip any remaining PII using the redaction checklist.
3. For each record, assign `human_score` and set `labeler_id` to your analyst alias.
4. Replace `ground_truth_score` with `human_score` (or add `human_score` alongside it).
5. Add `labeled_at: "YYYY-MM-DD"`.
6. Submit records for peer review by a second analyst (inter-annotator agreement target: κ ≥ 0.70).
7. Merge approved records into `rationale_judge_holdout.yaml`.
8. Run `python -m pytest tests/apps_underwriting_ai/test_ds_r1_holdout_dataset.py -q`.
9. When the validator passes, notify the engineering team to unblock DS-R2.

## DS-R1 Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] `rationale_judge_holdout.yaml` has ≥ 100 records.
- [ ] Each of the 5 dimensions has ≥ 20 records.
- [ ] All records have `labeler_id` set to a real analyst alias (not `synthetic_*`).
- [ ] At least one second-analyst peer review completed.
- [ ] `test_ds_r1_holdout_dataset.py` passes (all tests green).
- [ ] No PII present (compliance sign-off on file).
- [ ] Engineering team notified; DS-R2 unblocked.
