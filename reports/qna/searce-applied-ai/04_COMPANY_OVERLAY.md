## Company brief (from deep research)
Searce is a process-first, AI-native modern technology consultancy founded in 2004 by Hardik Parekh, headquartered in Pune, India with executive presence in Houston, Texas. ~2,200 employees, ~$126M revenue (2025), entirely bootstrapped — a rare profile at this scale. Originally a BPO and process-consulting firm, it pivoted in the mid-2010s to become a premier Google Cloud Platform implementation partner, expanded to AWS/Databricks/Snowflake by the early 2020s, and rebranded its entire portfolio as "AI-native" in 2024. Searce's neologism "futurify" anchors marketing; the proprietary "evlos" methodology (the word SOLVE backwards) anchors execution — reverse-engineering from desired outcome to foundational code, redesigning business processes BEFORE overlaying technology. Internal HAPPIER values (Humble, Adaptable, Positive, Passionate, Innovative, Excellence, Responsible) drive cultural fit screening; the DRI (Directly Responsible Individual) model assigns absolute accountability to senior leaders for revenue + delivery + architecture, with no hand-offs. External recognition is strong (Google Cloud Premier Partner, Everest Major Contender 2025); employee sentiment data shows operational friction in middle management, compensation, and offshore delivery centers — material context for any senior hire's first-90-day plan.




## Industry trends shaping the role
- 2026 inflection: AI moves from isolated experiments to AI-native enterprise architectures
- Context Engineering replacing Prompt Engineering as the primary lever — chunking, metadata, semantic search
- Agentic RAG and Graph-Based RAG over flat vector similarity for multi-hop reasoning
- Model Context Protocol (MCP) emerging as the zero-trust standard for LLM tool integration
- Layered Memory Design (short-term + episodic + semantic + procedural) becoming table stakes for true agents
- AI Gateways + FinOps: model routing by task complexity, rate limiting, prompt-injection guardrails
- Multi-Agent A2A architectures over single monolithic models — Agent Development Kits formalizing this pattern
