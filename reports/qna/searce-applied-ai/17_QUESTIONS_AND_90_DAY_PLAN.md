## LIVE VERBAL-FIRST OVERWRITE

These cards are optimized for live interview readout.

### Always-on output rules
- Use **short clauses** and a natural spoken cadence.
- Prefer **bullets** for architecture, governance, STAR, RCA, and technical answers.
- Limit live answers to **4 to 5 top-level bullets** unless Amit explicitly asks for depth.
- Use sub-bullets only when they make the answer easier to read under pressure.
- Use **bold** for words that should carry weight.
- Use *italics* for softer emphasis, contrast, or pacing.
- Do not over-format.
- Avoid forced summary endings such as polished "that is the difference between" lines.
- End on the practical implication or final control point.

### First-person credibility
Use lightly and naturally:
- "What I have seen in my agentic work..."
- "What I have learned building agentic workflows..."
- "My bias is to design the failure mode first..."

### Tight routing rule
- Architecture stays architecture-first.
- Governance stays risk/control-first.
- Do not drift into STAR, DGS, ROI, or 90-day plan unless asked.

### Inline citation discipline
- Every claim about Searce that came from research must carry a `[S#]` tag resolved in card 19 (Source Register).
- Personal experience (STAR, RCA) needs no citation.
- If you cannot cite a research claim, downgrade the framing to "my read is" or drop it.

### Preferred reliability-chain phrasing
When a reliability chain is useful, use these clusters instead of a long control inventory:
- **trusted data and semantic grounding**
- **validated execution and model lifecycle**
- **governed action and policy gates**
- **observability, audit, and regression evals**

## Questions to ask the interviewer(s)

### Questions for Vrinda Khurjekar
- Of your five Sept 2025 priorities — customer partnerships, scaling AI-native offerings, Americas footprint, partner ecosystem, high-performance teams — which one are you most worried about for FY26, and what would 'great' look like by end of Q2?
- Where do you draw the line between your Americas P&L and this practice's P&L? What's the operating cadence you'd want with me — weekly pipeline review, monthly business review, quarterly leadership review?
- You've talked publicly about the offshore-onshore friction in delivery. What authority does this role have over the levers — hiring, leveling, comp, work geography — to address that without breaking the practice's velocity?
- Tell me about a North America AI deal in the last year that worked — what made it land — and one that didn't, and what we learned.
- How does the partner motion with Google Cloud actually work today? What's the gap between 'partner of the year' optics and dollars in the joint pipeline?
- What's the Searce IP commercialization model? When does a successful client win become a productized accelerator, and who owns that conversion?
- You're 17 years in. What's the cultural failure mode I most need to avoid in my first 90 days — the thing that's killed previous senior hires?
- Paul Pallath leads Applied AI on the technical side; Ram Devaraj on engineering. How do you envision the three of us working together, and where would you expect me to lean on each of them?




## Question-asking principles
- Ask questions that show you have already thought about the operating model.
- Ask one question that probes their **failure mode** ("what would make the first 90 days obviously fail?").
- Ask one question that probes the **decision rights** ("who owns the architecture decision when product and platform disagree?").
- Do not ask anything you can find on their website.

## 90-day plan

### Days 1–30 — Listen and instrument
- Listening tour with Vrinda + Siddharth + Paul Pallath + Ram Devaraj — understand the operating cadences, the seam-points, and the cultural land mines.
- Account-by-account triage of the existing Americas Applied AI pipeline with Vrinda — jointly identify the 3 highest-leverage pursuits to win in the first six months.
- Audit current Applied AI delivery patterns and IP — what's reusable today, what's bespoke, what's at delivery risk. Output: a one-page IP-vs-bespoke heatmap.
- Customer listening: 5–7 CxO conversations at active and target accounts before proposing anything — EVLOS-style listening, not pitching.
- Reconnect with Google Cloud Americas account team(s) — refresh co-sell alignment with Vrinda's joint roadmap.


### Days 31–60 — Pilot and pattern
- Ship v1 of the Applied AI accelerator catalog — 3–5 reference architectures (agentic workflow, GraphRAG retrieval, AI gateway, layered memory, governed write path) with deal-sheet pricing and partner-distribution alignment.
- Convert 1–2 active pursuits into funded multi-quarter programs with explicit ROI gates and joint Google Cloud co-funding where applicable.
- Stand up Applied AI delivery standards: evaluation gates, observability instrumentation, FinOps cost ceilings, HITL escalation thresholds. Same artifact across delivery teams.
- Hire 2–3 senior architects + a delivery lead — DRI-shaped JDs, no 'AI evangelist' roles, comp aligned with Vrinda's authority on the lever.
- Joint partner business review with Google Cloud Americas team — align on Searce's Applied AI co-sell pursuits for the next two quarters.


### Days 61–90 — Productize and propagate
- First Applied AI program live in production at a strategic Americas account, with measurable outcomes published to Vrinda + Hardik.
- Repeatable practice playbook documented end-to-end — listening → pursuit shaping → delivery → IP capture loop.
- Partner program v1 with Google Cloud Americas — 2+ funded joint pursuits in motion, quarterly cadence locked.
- Operating-model recommendations to Vrinda for delivery friction — specific authority requests (hiring/leveling/comp), offshore-onshore handoff standards, and named senior hires to drive each.
- Practice scorecard v1 with Vrinda: pipeline coverage, win rate, productized-revenue mix, accelerator adoption, customer NPS, team retention. Aligned weekly cadence.
