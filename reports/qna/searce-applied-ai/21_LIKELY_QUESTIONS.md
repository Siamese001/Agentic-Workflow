## LIVE VERBAL-FIRST OVERWRITE

These cards are optimized for live interview readout.

### Always-on output rules
- Use **short clauses** and a natural spoken cadence.
- Prefer **bullets** for architecture, governance, STAR, RCA, and technical answers.
- Limit live answers to **4 to 5 top-level bullets** unless Amit explicitly asks for depth.
- Use sub-bullets only when they make the answer easier to read under pressure.
- Use **bold** for words that should carry weight.
- Use *italics* for softer emphasis, contrast, or pacing.
- Do not over-format.
- Avoid forced summary endings such as polished "that is the difference between" lines.
- End on the practical implication or final control point.

### First-person credibility
Use lightly and naturally:
- "What I have seen in my agentic work..."
- "What I have learned building agentic workflows..."
- "My bias is to design the failure mode first..."

### Tight routing rule
- Architecture stays architecture-first.
- Governance stays risk/control-first.
- Do not drift into STAR, DGS, ROI, or 90-day plan unless asked.

### Inline citation discipline
- Every claim about Searce that came from research must carry a `[S#]` tag resolved in card 19 (Source Register).
- Personal experience (STAR, RCA) needs no citation.
- If you cannot cite a research claim, downgrade the framing to "my read is" or drop it.

### Preferred reliability-chain phrasing
When a reliability chain is useful, use these clusters instead of a long control inventory:
- **trusted data and semantic grounding**
- **validated execution and model lifecycle**
- **governed action and policy gates**
- **observability, audit, and regression evals**



## Purpose
A predicted-questions list, grouped by route, derived from the JD, role, and research. Use this as a rehearsal warmup, not as a script.



## Route: executive_fit
- Walk me through your first 90 days as Applied AI Practice Leader at Searce — what would you build, what would you measure, and how would you partner with me?
- Why Searce, why now? What about our motion is harder/different from where you've worked before?
- How do you translate ambiguous CxO demand into a fundable AI program with measurable outcomes?
- How do you navigate the DRI model — owning revenue, architecture, AND delivery with no hand-offs to offshore?
- What's the line between this role's P&L and my Americas P&L? How do we partner so neither of us is in every deal?
- How do you build genuine customer trust at the CxO level — not transactional, not deal-flow?



## Route: productization
- Scaling AI-native offerings is my #2 priority for the next phase. What does your accelerator catalog look like, and how do you decide what becomes IP vs. stays bespoke?
- How do you convert successful client engagements into repeatable IP that field teams can actually sell and delivery teams can actually execute?
- How do you price and package an AI offering for the regulated-industries buyer? What's the deal cycle and what's the comp model?
- $22M productized AI revenue at Unify — what was the formula? What made the IP reusable rather than another bespoke deliverable?
- How do you balance margin discipline with the investment needed to build durable IP?



## Route: global_engineering
- My #5 priority is building and empowering high-performance teams. What's your hiring playbook for senior AI architects — your network, your bar, your interview rubric?
- How do you operate as a practice leader with distributed global delivery? Searce's offshore model has friction — how would you reduce it without breaking velocity?
- How do you scale an AI engineering org from a small team to 25+ specialists while maintaining quality and culture?
- What's your view on onshore-offshore handoff standards? Where does the line move as a program scales?
- What's your approach to leveling, comp, and retention for top-tier AI engineering talent? What authority do you need from me to make this work?



## Route: ds_to_platform
- How do you take an AI pilot from notebook to production in a regulated enterprise environment — what are the actual gates?
- What does AI CI/CD look like in your world — evaluation, rollback, drift, observability — and how does it land at a Fortune 500 financial services client?
- How do you operationalize AI evaluation pipelines that survive model swaps and prompt drift over time?
- You've said cycle time matters — what's your story on lab-to-production compression?



## Route: star_proof
- Tell me about a time you built deep customer trust at the CxO level. What did you do that someone else might not have?
- STAR: a CxO conversation that turned an ambiguous problem into a funded multi-quarter AI program
- STAR: you had to build a practice from scratch — pipeline, IP, team, partner motion. What was the playbook? What broke and how did you fix it?
- STAR: a hyperscaler co-sell motion you ran end-to-end. What did the partner side actually need from you?
- STAR: a regulated-industries deal where governance/compliance was a deal-blocker. How did you unblock it?



## Route: rca
- Tell me about a production AI failure or a deal that fell apart — root cause, recovery, what changed about how you operated afterward.
- What's a hire that didn't work out, and what did you learn about your screening rubric?
- What's a partner-motion that misfired? Where did the alignment break?



## Route: governance
- Healthcare and financial services demand strict governance — how do you stand up the gates without slowing the deal cycle?
- What's your view on the human-in-the-loop boundary for high-stakes regulated workflows?
- How do you instrument trace integrity and audit trail in a way that survives a regulatory exam?



## Route: architecture
- At a high level — not Ram-deep — how would you architect the platform spine that lets us deliver Applied AI consistently across the practice?
- What's your view on Graph RAG vs. vector RAG for enterprise use cases? When does the complexity pay off?
- How does MCP fit into the partner-distributable accelerators we'd build?



## Route: cross_exam
- Defend your $22M productized revenue claim — walk me through the deal mix and the IP economics
- Defend your 8→28 team-scaling claim — what was the attrition, what was the leveling spread, what made it sustainable?
- Defend your hyperscaler partner-revenue claim — was that real co-sell or marketing line?
