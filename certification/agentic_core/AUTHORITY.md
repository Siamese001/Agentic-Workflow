## 3. OTEL Contradiction Resolution (RTC-REQ-020 + RTC-REQ-022)

`rtc_req_otel_replay_report.json` reports `RTC-REQ-020` and `RTC-REQ-022` as `BLOCKED`
with messages about a missing `otel_collector_receipt.json` and `otel_metric_delta_report.json`.
The final report simultaneously shows both as `SIGNED_OFF`. This is **not an internal contradiction**.
They reference two distinct evidence pathways:

### Pathway 1 — OTEL Collector Receipt Sub-Claim (`rtc_req_otel_replay_report.json`)

This sub-claim requires an **external OTEL collector** (`docker-compose.otel.yml`) to produce
`otel_collector_receipt.json` and `otel_metric_delta_report.json`. Neither artifact is on disk.
This pathway is **BLOCKED** and remains so. It is a separate, additive sub-claim — its absence
does not invalidate the SIGNED_OFF rows.

### Pathway 2 — apps_rg Runtime OTEL Trace Evidence (authoritative — backs SIGNED_OFF)

| Req | Assertion IDs | Artifact |
|---|---|---|
| `RTC-REQ-020` | `ASRT-27373b6fb09494d2...`, `ASRT-473ff052ae80e1ba...`, `ASRT-8eb20d3401003245...`, `ASRT-ee597e3be407beba...` | `artifacts/certification/runtime/RTC-REQ-020/apps_rg_runtime_observability_020_evidence.json` |
| `RTC-REQ-022` | `ASRT-2a6ea19e458cf87e...`, `ASRT-2c6e068bf94fb9b5...`, `ASRT-5834be49b07693607...`, `ASRT-ac68b115d6ec8d87...` | `artifacts/certification/runtime/RTC-REQ-022/apps_rg_observability_022_evidence.json` |

All 8 assertions pass `verifier_pass`, `verifier_exit_zero`, `last_verified_timestamp`, and
`otel_trace` controls. **These are the evidence rows that back the SIGNED_OFF status.**

### Resolution

The apparent contradiction is resolved by scope: `rtc_req_otel_replay_report.json` covers the
external-collector sub-claim only. The SIGNED_OFF determination is backed by Pathway 2. The
collector receipt sub-claim is deferred — documented in §4 below.
