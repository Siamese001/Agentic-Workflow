Data Scientist - Machine Learning Platform
Company: DataMind Analytics
Location: New York, NY / Hybrid
Salary: $125,000 - $165,000 + Performance Bonus + Benefits

About DataMind:
DataMind Analytics is a fast-growing AI/ML company revolutionizing how enterprises leverage machine learning for business intelligence. Our platform processes billions of data points daily to deliver actionable insights through advanced predictive models and real-time analytics.

Position Overview:
We're looking for a talented Data Scientist to join our Machine Learning Platform team. You'll work on cutting-edge ML problems, develop predictive models, and build scalable data pipelines that power our analytics platform. This role combines deep technical expertise with business impact.

Key Responsibilities:
• Design and implement machine learning models for classification, regression, and clustering problems
• Develop and optimize data processing pipelines using Python, Spark, and cloud technologies
• Conduct exploratory data analysis to identify patterns and insights from large datasets
• Build and maintain feature engineering pipelines for model training and inference
• Collaborate with product and engineering teams to deploy models to production
• Monitor model performance and implement retraining strategies
• Create data visualizations and reports to communicate findings to stakeholders
• Research and evaluate new ML techniques and technologies

Required Qualifications:
• 3+ years of experience in data science or machine learning engineering
• Strong programming skills in Python and experience with ML libraries (scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch)
• Solid foundation in statistics, linear algebra, and machine learning algorithms
• Experience with big data technologies (Spark, Hadoop, or similar)
• Proficiency in SQL and experience with relational and NoSQL databases
• Knowledge of data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, or Python libraries)
• Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure) for ML workloads
• Strong understanding of software engineering best practices and version control

Preferred Qualifications:
• PhD or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related field
• Experience with deep learning and neural networks
• Knowledge of MLOps practices and model deployment frameworks
• Familiarity with time series analysis and forecasting
• Experience with A/B testing and experimental design
• Background in natural language processing or computer vision
• Publications in ML conferences or journals

Technical Stack:
• Languages: Python, R, SQL
