# Work Experience Template

## Experience Section Framework

### Position Header Format
**[Job Title]** | [Company Name] | [Location]
[Start Date] – [End Date/Present]

### Experience Description Structure

#### Achievement-Focused Bullet Points
- **Action Verb** + **What You Did** + **How/Why** + **Quantifiable Result**
- **STAR Method**: Situation, Task, Action, Result
- **Impact Language**: Focus on outcomes and business value
- **Skill Integration**: Weave technical and soft skills naturally

#### Key Components for Each Position
1. **Core Responsibilities**: Scope of role and primary functions
2. **Major Achievements**: Quantifiable impacts and results
3. **Leadership/Initiative**: Projects led or improvements initiated
4. **Skill Application**: How technical/soft skills were applied
5. **Business Impact**: Revenue, efficiency, or quality improvements

## Experience Optimization Guidelines

### Content Best Practices
1. **Start with Strong Action Verbs**: Use powerful verbs that demonstrate impact
2. **Quantify Everything Possible**: Include numbers, percentages, dollar amounts
3. **Focus on Results, Not Responsibilities**: Emphasize outcomes over duties
4. **Tailor to Target Role**: Highlight experiences most relevant to target position
5. **Show Career Progression**: Demonstrate growth and increasing responsibility

### Formatting Standards
- **Consistent Structure**: Same format for all positions
- **Bullet Points**: 3-6 bullet points per position maximum
- **Tense Usage**: Present tense for current role, past tense for previous roles
- **Length**: 2-3 lines per bullet point maximum
- **Keywords**: Include relevant industry and role-specific keywords

### ATS Optimization
- **Standard Section Headings**: Use "Professional Experience" or "Work Experience"
- **Clear Formatting**: Avoid tables, columns, or complex formatting
- **Keyword Inclusion**: Integrate job description keywords naturally
- **Company Names**: Use official company names without abbreviations
- **Date Consistency**: Use consistent date format (MM/YYYY or Month YYYY)

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Passive Voice**: "Was responsible for" → "Managed" or "Led"
- **Vague Statements**: "Improved processes" → "Streamlined workflow reducing processing time by 25%"
- **Job Description Copy**: Don't simply copy job descriptions
- **Irrelevant Details**: Focus on experiences relevant to target role
- **Excessive Jargon**: Use industry-standard terms but avoid unnecessary complexity
- **Typos and Grammar**: Proofread carefully for errors

### Experience Gap Handling
- **Honesty**: Never fabricate or exaggerate experience
- **Focus on Learning**: Emphasize skills gained during gaps
- **Contract/Freelance Work**: Include relevant independent projects
- **Volunteer Work**: Include significant volunteer leadership roles
- **Continuing Education**: Highlight relevant courses and certifications during gaps
