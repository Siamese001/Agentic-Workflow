# **Executive Intelligence Briefing: Strategic Resume Generation for the Head of Agentic Engineering**

## **Strategic Context and Executive Mandate**

The financial services sector is currently undergoing a foundational transformation, pivoting from traditional deterministic software engineering toward probabilistic, autonomous, and goal-oriented systems—collectively known as agentic artificial intelligence. Truist Financial Corporation, maintaining a substantial market share across high-growth markets in the United States, is actively operationalizing AI across its enterprise architecture to drive digital transformation and operational excellence.1 To lead this initiative, Truist has established "The Forge," an internal engineering hub designed to operate with the agility of a startup while leveraging the vast data resources of a top-tier regulated bank.3  
The Head of Agentic Engineering is positioned as the executive leader of the most strategically critical function within The Forge. This individual is tasked with designing, building, and operating the enterprise AI agents and agentic workflows that will define the bank's next-generation value proposition. To support the generation of a highly targeted executive resume for this specific job description (JD), this briefing deconstructs the operational, technical, and regulatory landscape surrounding the role. The resume must carefully navigate a profound paradox embedded within the JD: the demand for a cutting-edge "builder-leader" with deep expertise in Large Language Models (LLMs), multi-agent orchestration, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), juxtaposed against an uncompromising requirement for 20+ years of information security, cybersecurity, and financial regulatory risk management experience.  
Recent developments at Truist illuminate the urgency and scale of this initiative. In February 2026, the institution launched an AI-enabled receivables platform designed to accelerate cash application and minimize exceptions, utilizing machine learning and business rules to auto-match payments to invoices.4 This deployment signals that Truist has moved beyond conceptual AI experiments and is actively embedding autonomous logic into core financial operations. The candidate's resume must therefore project the capacity to scale these early successes into a ubiquitous, secure, and governed agentic ecosystem, aligning perfectly with the priorities of the executive leadership team, including Chief Information Officer Steve Hagerman, Chief AI & Data Officer Pascal Belaud, and Chief Divisional Technology Officer Lo Li.1

## **Deconstructing the Job Description Paradox**

An analysis of the specific requirements for the Head of Agentic Engineering reveals a fascinating duality that must serve as the structural foundation of the candidate's resume. While the primary responsibilities focus on software engineering, multi-agent orchestration, and prompt architecture, the mandatory qualifications listed in the JD are overwhelmingly focused on cybersecurity.  
The required qualifications explicitly mandate two decades of experience in financial services, government, or large consulting firms, specifically focused on information security risk management. Furthermore, the role requires fifteen years of experience leading issue management and resolving financial regulatory and internal risk partner findings, alongside extensive knowledge of cybersecurity capabilities such as Identity and Access Management (IAM), encryption, and cloud security. Deep knowledge of frameworks like NIST, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, SOX, and GLBA is considered foundational. Conversely, the preferred qualifications request twelve years of progressive software or AI engineering experience, with deep technical expertise in AI agent systems, prompt engineering, RAG, and evaluation for production AI systems.  
This structural anomaly yields a critical strategic insight for resume formulation: Truist views the deployment of autonomous AI agents primarily as a massive cybersecurity and regulatory risk vector that must be managed by a seasoned risk executive who also happens to possess deep engineering capabilities. An AI agent is granted agency to execute API calls, access databases, and trigger workflows independently. If an LLM is compromised via a prompt injection attack, the financial and reputational damage to the bank could be catastrophic. Therefore, the resume must not frame the candidate merely as an innovative developer, but rather as a defensive architect capable of enforcing safe, reliable, observable, and enterprise-worthy deployments.7

## **Mapping Experience to the Technical Stack**

The Head of Agentic Engineering is responsible for evaluating, selecting, and integrating the optimal enterprise agent platforms to support Truist's business objectives. The JD explicitly cites familiarity with Microsoft Copilot Studio, Azure AI Foundry, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, and Google Vertex AI Agent Engine. The resume must articulate hands-on leadership experience across these specific ecosystems, demonstrating an understanding of their comparative strengths in a regulated enterprise context.

### **Microsoft Azure AI Foundry and Copilot Studio**

Microsoft provides a deeply integrated ecosystem that spans low-code interfaces and complex backend orchestration. Copilot Studio allows organizations to build conversational agents that retrieve real-time data from external APIs and automate repetitive tasks using natural language or visual designers.19 Furthermore, Microsoft has recently made agentic capabilities generally available within Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, allowing Copilot to take multi-step, app-native actions that drive high user engagement and retention.12  
For the underlying engineering architecture, Azure AI Foundry is critical. It supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), a vendor-neutral standard that allows developers to register enterprise APIs once and expose them securely to multiple agents across different frameworks.8 The resume should detail experience utilizing Azure AI Foundry to deploy multi-agent workflows using Semantic Kernel or AutoGen, emphasizing how the platform's native integration with Microsoft Entra ID enforces Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls. A strong resume bullet point would describe the orchestration of a multi-agent system where telemetry and observability services were extended with agent-aware instrumentation, tracing conversations and surfacing anomalies in real time to satisfy audit requirements.8

### **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore**

Amazon's approach with Bedrock AgentCore is highly modular, focusing on providing a secure, model-agnostic execution environment that allows developers to use any open-source framework. AgentCore provides essential enterprise capabilities such as the Runtime execution environment, tool governance through the Gateway module, and integration with Identity and Memory management.10  
In a financial services context, the observability architecture of AgentCore is a distinct advantage. The resume should reflect experience utilizing AgentCore to standardize execution platforms, preventing development teams from building custom, fragile scaffolding for every proof-of-concept.23 The candidate should articulate how they integrated CloudWatch logs and metrics emitted by the AgentCore Runtime and Gateway into enterprise monitoring platforms like Datadog.24 This specific detail demonstrates the ability to correlate agentic behavior with platform-level infrastructure signals, simplifying the debugging of complex interactions and ensuring that tool governance remains uncompromised at scale.

### **Google Vertex AI Agent Engine**

Vertex AI Agent Engine positions itself as a unified platform for AI system lifecycle management, consolidating model access, orchestration, and governance under a single control plane to prevent operational drift.25 The platform provides a governed runtime environment that includes robust context management, session memory banks, and a secure code sandbox for executing dynamic logic.26  
When detailing experience with the Google ecosystem, the resume must emphasize the utilization of native agent identities to enforce strict security boundaries.27 The narrative should highlight the ability to future-proof AI portfolios by leveraging Vertex AI's architectural flexibility, allowing the engineering team to swap underlying foundational models based on evolving cost, performance, or regulatory expectations while maintaining consistent, platform-level governance controls.25

## **Aligning Experience with Regulatory Frameworks**

The most complex challenge for the Head of Agentic Engineering will be ensuring that all technological innovations adhere to the stringent regulatory frameworks governing financial institutions. The resume must transition from listing general cybersecurity skills to articulating specific strategies for navigating the modern AI regulatory landscape.

### **The Shift from SR 11-7 to SR 26-2**

For over a decade, the Federal Reserve and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency governed financial models through the Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management (SR 11-7).15 However, the rapid proliferation of artificial intelligence necessitated a regulatory overhaul. In April 2026, the agencies rescinded SR 11-7, replacing it with SR 26-2, which explicitly extends core risk management principles to Generative AI and agentic systems.15  
The resume must explicitly demonstrate experience operationalizing the mandates of SR 26-2. The candidate should describe their leadership in implementing risk-based tailoring, ensuring that all AI agents are classified into tiers based on inherent risk, exposure, and purpose.17 A critical resume bullet should detail the development of a unified lifecycle approach, where development, validation, deployment, and monitoring are treated as a single governed chain with an unbroken lineage of data and logic, satisfying supervisory expectations.17 Furthermore, the resume must highlight experience constructing automated telemetry pipelines for continuous monitoring, tracking performance drift, data drift, and agent stability against hardcoded thresholds mapped directly to the agent's risk tier.17

### **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**

Complementing federal banking regulations is the National Institute of Standards and Technology AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI RMF 1.0), which provides a socio-technical approach to managing AI risks.13 The resume should map the candidate's past engineering initiatives directly to the four core functions of the NIST framework: Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.  
The narrative must project an understanding that AI risks extend beyond technical vulnerabilities to encompass complex social, legal, and ethical implications.13 The candidate should articulate how they embedded the seven characteristics of trustworthy AI—valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable and interpretable, privacy-enhanced, and fair—into their CI/CD pipelines and deployment protocols.14 Familiarity with the Financial Services (FS) AI RMF, an industry-led expansion featuring 230 specific Control Objectives designed for financial institutions, should also be explicitly noted, as it serves as the operational playbook for mapping agentic engineering practices to audit expectations.30
