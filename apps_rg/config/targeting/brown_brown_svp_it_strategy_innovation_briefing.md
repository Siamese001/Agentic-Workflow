# **Strategic Resume Preparation Analysis: Senior Vice President of IT Strategy & Innovation at Brown & Brown**

## **Executive Leadership Alignment and Organizational Context**

An executive candidate pursuing the role of Senior Vice President (SVP) of IT Strategy & Innovation at Brown & Brown must position their professional trajectory relative to a newly established enterprise technology cabinet.1 This strategic leadership position reports directly to the Chief Information Technology Officer (CITO), Dorothea "Dori" Henderson, who assumed office on February 23, 2026\.3 Henderson joined Brown & Brown following a successful tenure as Senior Vice President and Chief Digital Information Officer at CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield and an eighteen-year operational foundation in cybersecurity, global networks, and cloud engineering at Collins Aerospace.3 Her primary corporate mandate is to introduce rigorous operational discipline, accelerate digital capabilities, and drive unified data and artificial intelligence (AI) strategies across the enterprise.3  
Simultaneously, the candidate must demonstrate strong collaborative alignment with Chief Data Officer (CDO) Mike Vaughan.6 Vaughan is a vocal advocate of "data-forward" corporate cultures, actively steering the company away from academic concepts of technology, such as pure data mesh architectures, toward pragmatic business outcomes that drive revenue growth and expand enterprise margins.6 The candidate's resume must therefore reflect a history of translating sophisticated enterprise architecture (EA) models into direct economic value rather than showcasing isolated technical milestones.1  
To optimize high-signal resume positioning, the candidate should anchor their historical accomplishments against the financial baseline of Brown & Brown, which delivered $5.9 billion in total revenues in 2025, representing a 22.8% total growth rate.8 However, this growth was primarily driven by aggressive mergers and acquisitions (M\&A).8 Organic revenue growth remained moderate at 2.8% for the full year 2025 8, decreased by 2.8% in the fourth quarter of 2025 10, and remained flat during the first quarter of 2026\.9 Consequently, the strategic focus of the C-suite centers heavily on leveraging technology to drive organic lead generation, optimize operational workflows, and enhance teammate productivity.8

## **Post-Merger Technology Integration and Federated Architecture**

The decentralized operating model of Brown & Brown requires the SVP of IT Strategy & Innovation to lead the Enterprise Architecture function with an emphasis on flexibility and interoperability rather than rigid centralization.1 Standard corporate architecture strategies often fail in decentralized brokerages because they disrupt local customer service models and entrepreneurial agility.12 A successful executive resume must highlight the design and implementation of federated enterprise architecture systems.1 These frameworks must establish security, data privacy, and interoperability standards while allowing decentralized units to choose the applications that optimize their local markets.1  
This architectural balance is particularly critical during post-merger technology integrations.3 In August 2025, Brown & Brown completed the acquisition of RSC Topco (Accession), the largest transaction in company history, absorbing Risk Strategies and One80 Intermediaries.8 This transaction brought in over 5,500 new teammates and led to the creation of a new Specialty Distribution segment.8 Integrating massive platforms like Accession—while continuing to onboard niche bolt-on agencies like American Adventure Insurance or Claim Technologies—demands repeatable, low-friction integration strategies.11  
The candidate's resume should highlight experience in:

* **EBITDA Synergy Realization**: Engineering automated, repeatable technology integration playbooks that helped capture projected post-acquisition cost savings, such as the $30 million to $40 million in EBITDA synergies targeted in the Accession transaction.9  
* **Decentralized Post-Merger Cyber Due Diligence**: Establishing clear cybersecurity and data-handling standards to quickly assess, secure, and integrate acquired IT environments without delaying transition timelines.16  
* **Modern Integration Ecosystems**: Designing modular, API-first architectures that connect disparate agency systems, third-party vertical software, and core enterprise records—such as Workday, SAP, HubSpot CRM, and Microsoft Dynamics Business Central—into cohesive automated environments.18

## **Cultural Alignment and Professional Narrative**

In addition to demonstrating technical and strategic capability, the candidate's executive narrative must align with Brown & Brown's distinct corporate culture.1 The company does not use the term "employees," referring to its workforce as "teammates" and its leadership team as "leaders" rather than "managers".15 The enterprise operates as a performance-driven meritocracy that rewards self-starters, hard work, and professional achievement.15 This teammate-centric philosophy is reinforced by a shared equity model, with approximately 20% of the company owned by teammates through programs like the Teammate Stock Purchase Plan (TSPP).15  
The candidate should structure their resume accomplishments using active, results-oriented verbs and phrasing that matches this culture:

* Use phrases like **"built organizational capability," "empowered cross-functional teammates," "fostered a culture of collaborative experimentation," and "mentored high-performing technology leaders"**.1  
* Incorporate the symbolic attributes of the company's icon, the cheetah—**vision, swiftness, strength, and agility**—by framing achievements around proactive problem-solving, rapid deployment times, and adaptable strategies under pressure.15  
* Demonstrate the ability to translate complex technology concepts into clear, compelling business narratives for all stakeholders, from frontline engineers to business unit leaders and board members.1

## **Strategic Recommendations for Interview Preparation**

To complement a tailored resume, the candidate should prepare for executive interviews by focusing on several strategic areas:

* **Articulate the Role of Technology in a Decentralized Model**: Be prepared to discuss how to introduce enterprise architecture standards across independent profit centers without creating operational friction.1 The candidate should explain how they plan to engage business leaders in hands-on sessions to demonstrate how data-forward initiatives can directly improve their margins and organic revenue.6  
* **Frame AI as a Tool to Support Talent**: Explain how technology can be used to improve productivity and scale teammate capability.7 The candidate should articulate a clear approach to investing in talent development and technical training, ensuring that automated systems are designed to support and enhance human expertise.1  
* **Demonstrate Post-Merger Synergy Delivery**: Highlight experience in coordinating integrations for complex acquisitions.9 Be ready to discuss the specific methodologies used to assess technology portfolios, manage cybersecurity risks, and transition diverse technical teams into a unified segment.9  
* **Address Regulatory and Security Risks**: Discuss the balance between innovation and compliance in regulated markets.22 Frame cybersecurity and AI risk management as essential business practices that protect deal timing, valuation, and customer trust.7
